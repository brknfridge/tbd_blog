    04 — Method
    
How the number is built

    
    
      
  • Start with the adult population of the city, split by single year of age and sex.
    • 
      
  • Keep only people who are single — never married, divorced or widowed — at each age.
    • 
      
  • Apply each criterion as a share of that pool: height from a normal curve by sex, income from an age-adjusted earnings curve for the city, community from mother-tongue shares, religion from census shares (within each community you pick, where you pick one), and habits from national survey prevalence.
    • 
      
  • Weeks assume you date at random from singles of your chosen sex and age range in the city, one a week, never the same person twice.
    • 
    

    
Modelled on public sources — Census of India, NFHS-5, PLFS and published height studies — projected to 2026. Criteria are treated as independent. It does not account for whether they like you back. Treat the result as directional, not a headcount.
